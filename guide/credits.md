# Credits

These plugins are built on other people's work. Thank you to:

- **KOReader** (koreader.rocks, AGPL-3.0): the e-book reader these plugins run in, made by the KOReader community. Its own code does much of the work underneath: the screens and menus, gestures, reading statistics, Wikipedia's article download and the SSH client.
- **koreader-syncthing** by jasonchoimtt (github.com/jasonchoimtt/koreader-syncthing, AGPL-3.0): the Syncthing plugin is based on it.
- **Syncthing** (syncthing.net, MPL-2.0): the program that does the syncing, made by the Syncthing project. The release zip includes it, unmodified; its source is at github.com/syncthing/syncthing.
- **Dropbear** (matt.ucc.asn.au/dropbear): the SSH client that comes with KOReader, which the SSH plugin uses.
- **Lucide** (lucide.dev, ISC licence): the icons in every plugin.
- **Noto Sans** (SIL Open Font License) and **Droid Sans Mono** (Apache License 2.0): the fonts in this guide, with symbols from Microsoft's **Segoe UI Symbol**.
- **Wikipedia** and its editors: articles are Wikipedia's, under CC BY-SA.
- **Open-Meteo** (open-meteo.com, CC BY 4.0): the weather forecast and place search without Home Assistant. Weather data by Open-Meteo.com.
- The US **National Weather Service** (weather.gov): weather alerts in the US.
- The crosswords' publishers: puzzles are downloaded from their own websites and belong to them.

Home Assistant, Microsoft To Do, OneNote, Ollama and Claude are their makers' products; these plugins only connect to them.

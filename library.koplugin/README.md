# Library for KOReader

Your books and folders in one place, for KOReader on a Kindle: covers or a list, Home (continue reading, recent books, shelves), Reading, To read, Finished, authors, series and collections, sorted and grouped (and a collection made from a group); folders with Move, Copy, Rename and Delete (with Undo) and Select for several at once; search; and each book's page with its progress, status, reading statistics, what the book says about itself (series, publisher, date, subjects) and its file; and Find details online for books with few details (Open Library and Google Books, free and without an account: shown first, saved only on Save, with Undo). Books open in KOReader's reader, and Back from a book comes back to the Library.

It uses KOReader's own records: the Cover browser's titles and covers, each book's settings, the history, collections, folder shortcuts and the Reading statistics plugin. Library settings > Browse books with switches back to KOReader's file browser.

Part of the Kindle apps (the launcher, apps.koplugin, and the others); it works on its own too. See `docs/guide.md`.

Install: copy the `library.koplugin` folder into `koreader/plugins/` and restart KOReader.
